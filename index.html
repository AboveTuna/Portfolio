ABOUT THE STUDIO

Indie Game Studio

AboveTuna is an independent game studio based in South Africa, making cosy games people can enjoy with their friends.

Founded by game designer and programmer Dior Zaverdino, AboveTuna started as a hobby and grew into a full-time goal. We work across game design, programming, 3D art, animation and sound, building mainly in Unity, with experience in Unreal Engine too.
