ABOUT ME

Indie Game Developer

I'm Dior Zaverdino, a game designer and programmer based in South Africa. As a generalist I have experience in:
- Game design, programming, 3D art, animation, and sound design.
My primary engine is Unity, but I also have experience with Unreal Engine.

I've been an ambitious game designer since my early teens. What started as a hobby turned into a fulltime goal. That goal? Make cosy games people can enjoy with their friends.
